## Brainstorm Product Ideas (Existing Product)

Multi-perspective ideation for continuous product discovery. Confirm the objective and desired outcomes; ideate from PM, Designer, and Engineer viewpoints; prioritize ideas by strategic alignment, potential impact, feasibility/effort, and differentiation; then provide a concise description, selection rationale, and assumptions to validate for each retained idea.
