## Identify Assumptions (Existing Product)

Stress-test a feature from Product Manager, Designer, and Engineer perspectives. Identify Value, Usability, Viability, and Feasibility assumptions, state what could go wrong, confidence, and a suggested way to test. Strengthen the idea rather than merely rejecting it.
