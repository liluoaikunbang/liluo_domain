# Stop Slop

Eliminate predictable AI writing patterns from prose.

## Core Rules

1. Cut filler phrases and emphasis crutches.
2. Break formulaic structures and rhetorical setups.
3. Use active voice with a human subject where suitable.
4. Be specific rather than vague.
5. Put the reader in the room.
6. Vary rhythm.
7. Trust readers and state facts directly.
8. Cut pull-quote-like phrasing.

## Quick Checks

Check for filler, vague declarations, false contrast, repetitive sentence rhythm, unsupported rhetorical questions, inanimate agency, narrator distance, and unnecessary meta-joiners. Rewrite only when the issue harms the text.
