# Approval workflow

Candidate states: `proposed`, `accepted`, `accepted-with-changes`, `held`, `rejected`, `merged`, `expanded`, `written`.

1. Present concise `proposed` cards. The user may accept, revise, hold, reject, merge, request alternatives or change scope.
2. Never write formal story data at this stage. Persist only on explicit request.
3. Accepted cards may be expanded through the story-authoring Skill. Resolve remaining high-value choices through focused interview questions.
4. A card becomes `written` only after approved formal writing and must record the real story key.
5. Suppress identical rejected fingerprints for the same target. A rejection does not ban the whole topic unless the user says so. Held cards remain reviewable and unwritten.
