# Originality rules

Extract mechanisms, compare multiple sources, discard proper nouns and distinctive event order, then rewrite around 璃落 canon. Do not copy sentences, splice paragraphs, rename characters in reused prose, preserve signature dialogue, or use one work as a complete template.

Run `external:knowledge:copy-check` before formal writes. `low`, `medium`, and `high` are conservative writing warnings, not legal conclusions. Rewrite medium/high results and rerun until risk is low or explain the unresolved evidence without quoting long source text.

Never record real-world restraint instructions, force calculations, executable procedures, or operational harm guidance in cards.
